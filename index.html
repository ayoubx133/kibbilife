Kibbi Life: My Town World

SimulationPretend playAges 4+

Play pretend with Kibbi in a big cute town with 30 places to explore! Dress up Kibbi and her friends in the character maker, then walk around a busy town map and visit the home, salon, market, cafe, burger diner, bubble tea shop, police station, school, hospital, park and many more places. Drag friends anywhere, tap them to see them laugh, wave, jump and spin, cook, shop, play music, care for pets and decorate your own room.

  • Character maker with 5 body types, 30 hair styles, outfits and a color slider for every item
  • A painted town map with 30 places: 17 free and 13 extra places like the airport, fun park and beach resort
  • Welcome gifts for 7 days, a daily gift, quests, stickers and hidden surprises
  • Free to play, optional in-app purchases always behind a parent gate
