Kibbi Coloring Book

Brain & PuzzleNo adsAges 4+

Color 100 cute pages of Kibbi, her friends, pets and town with crayons!

Open Kibbi's coloring book and fill 100 happy pages with color! Kibbi, Tobi, Zuzu, little Mimi, Mochi the kitten and Biscuit the puppy are waiting on every page. Pick a crayon and tap a part to fill it, draw with the brush and stay inside the lines, add stripes, dots, hearts or stars, make things sparkle with glitter and drag cute stickers onto your picture. In Kibbi Town every part has a number, so match the crayon numbers to paint the street, the shops and the school bus. Every finished page gets a golden frame while Kibbi and her friends cheer, and it is saved in your own gallery to look at again. Five coloring books with 20 pages each: Kibbi and Friends, Pets, Kibbi Town, Party and Princess, and Dreams: Sea and Space.

  • 100 coloring pages in 5 books
  • Tap fill, brush, patterns, glitter and stickers
  • Color by number pages in Kibbi Town
  • Kibbi, her friends, Mochi and Biscuit on every page
  • Every finished page is framed and saved to your gallery
  • Calm music box music and soft crayon sounds
