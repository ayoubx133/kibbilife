Kibbi Nail Salon

SimulationNo adsAges 4+

Soak, scrub, file and paint pretty nails for Kibbi and her friends!

Welcome to Kibbi's nail salon! Kibbi, Zuzu, Tobi, little Mimi and friends from all over town sit down with an order card that shows the nails they dream of. Soak the hand in the bubble bowl, scrub the nails clean, file the long tips, then pick a polish and paint every nail with real brush strokes. Add dots, stripes, french tips, stickers and sparkly gems, and finish with the nail dryer for a glossy shine. Match the order card to earn 3 stars while Mochi the kitten naps on the counter and Biscuit the puppy watches. Five salon worlds with 20 orders each: Kibbi's Friends, Town Spa Day, Party Nails, Princess Nails and Rainbow Stars.

  • 100 nail orders in 5 salon worlds
  • Soak, scrub, file, polish, nail art and dryer steps
  • Paint with real strokes, 10 polish colors with glitter and pearl
  • Stickers, patterns and gems to drag onto every nail
  • Calm salon music and soft relaxing sounds
  • Helping hand shows little players what to do
