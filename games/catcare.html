Kibbi and Mochi Cat Care

SimulationNo adsAges 4+

Feed, bathe, brush and dress up Mochi the kitten and her cat friends!

Mochi the fluffy kitten needs you! Kibbi helps you take care of Mochi and her kitten friends Ginger, Smokey, Cookie, Snow, Peach and Luna. Look at the thought bubble and give the kitty the food it wants, rub in bubbles in the bath and rinse them away with the shower, rub the fur dry with a fluffy towel and brush out every knot. Wave a feather, a yarn ball or a toy mouse and watch the kitty jump and play, then pet its head softly until it falls asleep under a cozy blanket. Dress the cats up with bows, flower crowns, party hats and crowns, and in the last world decorate the cat house with beds, cat trees, pictures and toys. Five worlds with 20 levels each: Mochi's Day, Bubble Bath, Play Time, Fashion Kitty and Cat House.

  • 100 kitty care levels in 5 worlds
  • Feed, bubble bath, towel dry and brush
  • Play with feathers, yarn and toy mice
  • Pet the kitty to sleep for a cozy nap
  • Bows, crowns and party hats for every cat
  • Decorate the cat house with beds and cat trees
