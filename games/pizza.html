Kibbi Pizza Chef

SimulationNo adsAges 4+

Make, bake and deliver yummy pizzas for Kibbi and her friends!

Kibbi's pizza kitchen is open! Kibbi, Tobi, Zuzu, little Mimi, Mochi the kitten, Biscuit the puppy and friends from town come in with an order card that shows the pizza they dream of. Tap the soft dough to knead it, rub the rolling pin to roll it flat, pick tomato, pesto, white cream or BBQ sauce and swirl it on with the ladle, then sprinkle shredded cheese with your finger. Drag on pepperoni, mushrooms, olives, peppers, pineapple, basil, corn, shrimp and more, just like the order. Bake it in the cozy brick oven and take it out when it is perfectly golden and bubbly! Then cut it into slices along the lines, close the pizza box and ride the scooter through town to the customer's house. Mochi loves fish and shrimp, Biscuit wants sausage and ham! Five pizza worlds with 20 orders each: Pizza Kitchen, Dough School, Slice and Box, Scooter Delivery and the Pizza Party with heart shaped and half and half pizzas.

  • 100 pizza orders in 5 pizza worlds
  • Knead, roll, sauce, cheese, top, bake, slice and box
  • Swirl the sauce and sprinkle cheese with your finger
  • 17 toppings and heart shaped or half and half pizzas
  • Brick oven baking and scooter rides through town
  • Happy mandolin music and yummy kitchen sounds
