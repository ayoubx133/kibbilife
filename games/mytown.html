Kibbi Life: My Town World

SimulationBig worldAges 4+

Play pretend with Kibbi in a big cute town with 30 places to explore!

Meet Kibbi, a cheerful girl with a big polka dot bow, freckles and a strawberry bag, and build your own story in her colorful town. Dress up Kibbi and her friends in the character maker, then walk around a busy town map full of painted buildings and visit the home, salon, market, cafe, burger diner, bubble tea shop, police station, school, hospital, park and many more places. Drag friends anywhere, tap them to see them laugh, wave, jump and spin, cook, shop, play music, swim, care for pets and decorate your own room. Every place hides gifts, quests and stickers to collect, and a new welcome gift waits every day for the first week.

  • Character maker with 5 body types, 30 hair styles, jackets, socks and outfits, plus a color slider for every item
  • A big painted town map with 30 places to explore: 17 free and 13 extra places like the airport, fun park, snow lodge, beach resort and dream villa
  • Characters come alive: they breathe, blink, laugh, wave, jump, spin and walk
  • Make your own characters, save outfits in the wardrobe and decorate your room with themes and furniture
  • Welcome gifts for 7 days, a daily gift, quests, stickers, photo mode and hidden surprises
  • Seasonal town decorations for Halloween, Christmas and spring
