Kibbi Cake Bakery

SimulationNo adsAges 4+

Bake, frost and decorate cupcakes and cakes for Kibbi and her friends!

Kibbi's cake bakery is open! Kibbi, Tobi, Zuzu, little Mimi, Mochi the kitten, Biscuit the puppy and friends from town come in with an order card that shows the treat they dream of. Pick the flavor, whisk the batter round and round, hold to pour it up to the line and watch the oven: take the cake out when it is perfectly golden. Then pick a frosting color and swirl it on with your finger, stack sponge layers in the right order, pipe little cream roses along the edges and drag on sprinkles, strawberries, cherries, macarons, stars, candles and toppers. On birthday cakes you light every candle before the party can begin! Five bakery worlds with 20 orders each: Cupcake Corner, Birthday Cakes, Layer Cakes, Fruit and Cream and tall Dream Cakes.

  • 100 cake and cupcake orders in 5 bakery worlds
  • Whisk, pour, bake, frost, layer, pipe and decorate
  • Swirl frosting on with your finger in 10 colors
  • Sprinkles, fruit, macarons, candles, crowns and more
  • Light the birthday candles and build tall tier cakes
  • Happy swing piano music and soft kitchen sounds
