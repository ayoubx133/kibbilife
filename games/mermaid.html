Kibbi Mermaid Adventure

AdventureNo adsAges 4+

Swim as mermaid Kibbi, collect pearls, help sea friends and pick pretty tails!

Kibbi is a mermaid! Hold and drag your finger to swim through the sea, collect shiny pearls, pink shells and treasure bubbles, and bump gently into giggly jellyfish that only make Kibbi spin. Every swim has a sea friend who needs help: free a turtle stuck in the kelp, bring a lost baby fish home to its mama, or carry a shell back to a little crab. Zuzu, Tobi and Mimi swim along as merfriends, and Mochi the kitten and Biscuit the puppy cheer from their yellow bubble submarine. Before each swim, visit the Shell Wardrobe and pick a tail with scales, stars, glitter or rainbow stripes, a top and a crown, and make a friend's wish come true for bonus coins. Five sea worlds with 20 swims each: Coral Reef, Kelp Forest with tangly kelp, Sunken Ship with treasure chests and currents, Glow Caves with glowing pearls and puffer fish, and the Pearl Palace with a shy octopus and the crown pearl.

  • 100 swims in 5 sea worlds
  • Easy hold and drag swimming for little hands
  • Help a turtle, a baby fish and a crab in every world
  • Shell Wardrobe with 12 tails, 12 tops and 12 crowns
  • Gentle jellyfish and puffer fish, no game over ever
  • Happy steel drum island music and bubbly sounds
