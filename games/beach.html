Kibbi Beach Holiday

AdventureNo adsAges 4+

Pack, travel, surf, snorkel and build sand castles with Kibbi on holiday!

Kibbi is going on holiday! Every level is a new holiday day with fun things to do together with Tobi, Zuzu, little Mimi, Mochi the kitten and Biscuit the puppy. Fit everything into the suitcase like a puzzle, then drive, sail, fly or ride the train to the holiday spot. Rub sunscreen on everyone, dig up seashells in the sand, build sand castles with buckets and molds from the blueprint, surf the waves and jump to catch the stars, glide through the lagoon in a little snorkel submarine collecting pearls, serve ice cream at the beach stand, toast marshmallows at the campfire and snap a postcard when everybody smiles. Five holiday spots with 20 days each: Sunny Beach, Palm Island, Coral Lagoon, Seaside Town and Sunset Camp.

  • 100 holiday days in 5 holiday spots
  • Suitcase packing puzzles and fun trips by car, boat, plane, train and balloon
  • Sand castles, shell digging and sunscreen time
  • Surfing and a snorkel submarine with stars and pearls
  • Ice cream stand, campfire marshmallows and postcard photos
  • Sunny whistle soca music and happy beach sounds
