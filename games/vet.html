Kibbi Puppy Vet Hospital

SimulationNo adsAges 4+

Be the vet with Kibbi and help Biscuit, kittens, bunnies and ponies feel better!

Doctor Kibbi is ready in her white coat! Biscuit the puppy, Mochi the kitten and lots of animal friends come to the pet hospital with a patient card. Hold the thermometer to check for a fever, listen to the heartbeat with the stethoscope, clean dirty ears with a cotton swab, spray and clean a scrape and pick a cute bandage with hearts, stars or paws. Brush yucky spots off little teeth, give the right medicine from the card and move the x-ray around to find out what a hungry patient swallowed. At the end, every animal gets the treat it loves: a bone for puppies, a fish for kittens, a carrot for bunnies, seeds for hamsters and an apple for ponies. Five worlds with 20 patients each: Puppy Checkup, Kitten Clinic, Bunny and Hamster, X-Ray Lab and Pony Stables.

  • 100 animal patients in 5 hospital worlds
  • Thermometer, stethoscope, ear cleaning and teeth brushing
  • Clean scrapes and pick cute bandages
  • Find the swallowed toy with the x-ray
  • Learn which treat every animal loves
  • Bouncy chiptune music and happy animal sounds
