Kibbi Puzzle Match

Brain & PuzzleNo adsAges 4+

Swipe and match bows, strawberries and stars in 100 sweet puzzles with Kibbi!

Kibbi and her friends love puzzles! Swipe two neighbor tiles to line up 3 or more bows, strawberries, stars, cupcakes, hearts or flowers and watch them pop. Match 4 to make a ribbon that clears a whole line, match in an L or T shape to wrap a gift box that bursts, and match 5 to make a rainbow star that clears every tile of one kind. Swap two specials together for a super combo! Every level has goals to reach before the moves run out: collect tiles, clear pink frosting, open toy boxes, bring Mochi's yarn balls and Biscuit's bones down, break the ice and chase away the clouds. Big combos fill the magic wand, a free helper that pops any spot on the board. Kibbi, Zuzu, Tobi, little Mimi and the pets cheer for every match, and a friendly KEEP GOING gives extra moves so nobody ever loses. Five worlds with 20 levels each: Kibbi's Garden, Sweet Bakery, Toy Room, Ice Castle and Rainbow Sky, plus four tile styles to pick from.

  • 100 match 3 levels in 5 colorful worlds
  • Ribbons, gift boxes and rainbow stars made by your matches
  • Frosting, toy boxes, yarn balls, ice and clouds to clear
  • Magic wand helper earned with combos, no lives and no timers
  • Upbeat chiptune music and happy popping sounds
  • Helping hand shows little players their first move
