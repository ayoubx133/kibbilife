Kibbi Avatar World: Create & Play

SimulationBig worldAges 4+

Make your own characters, dress them up and play with Kibbi and friends!

Create anyone you can imagine in Kibbi Avatar World! Pick one of 5 body types and a skin tone, then style the face, eyes, brows, mouth, cheeks and makeup. Choose from 50 hairstyles and almost 200 clothes: tops, jackets, bottoms, dresses, animal suits, socks and shoes, plus hats, glasses, bags, wings, tails, ears and a little pet on the shoulder. Every item has color swatches and a color slider, so the looks never end. Undo, mirror and surprise buttons make it easy, and the wardrobe keeps your favorite outfits. Make up to 20 of your own characters and play with Kibbi, Tobi, Zuzu, little Mimi, Mochi the kitten and Biscuit the puppy. In the photo studio you pick a scene like the park, beach, stage, space or a castle, place up to 6 friends, choose poses and faces, add stickers and frames and keep your pictures in your album. Then decorate Kibbi's house room by room, visit Tobi's and Zuzu's houses and explore a cute town with a hair salon, boutique, school, park, cafe, beach, stage and pet shop.

  • Huge character creator: 5 body types, 50 hairstyles, almost 200 clothes, makeup, bags, wings, tails and ears
  • A color slider for every item, plus undo, mirror, surprise look and a 12 outfit wardrobe
  • Photo studio with 13 scenes, 10 poses, 7 faces, 20 stickers, 7 frames and your own photo album
  • Home builder: decorate every room with furniture, wall and floor colors and room themes
  • Kibbi and friends are ready to play, and you can make up to 20 of your own characters
  • Welcome gifts for 7 days, a daily gift, 99 quests and stickers, and a seasonal photo scene
