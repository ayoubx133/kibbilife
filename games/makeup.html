Kibbi Makeup Spa

SimulationNo adsAges 4+

Spa day and makeup looks for Kibbi, her friends and the town girls!

Welcome to Kibbi's makeup spa! Kibbi, Zuzu, little Mimi, Tobi and girls from town come in with a look card showing the spa treatment or the makeup they want. Rub on fluffy foam and splash it away, hold the warm steamer, paint on a peel-off mask and peel it off, rest cucumber slices on sleepy eyes and rub in soft cream. Then make them sparkle: brush on blush, paint the lips with real strokes, sweep pretty eyeshadow over the lids, add long or rainbow lashes and drag shiny face gems onto the dots. In the last world, paint hearts, stars, butterflies and moons on their cheeks. Mochi the kitten keeps you company and cheers for every finished look. Five worlds with 20 orders each: Spa Day, Sweet Looks, Eye Magic, Party Glam and Face Paint Fun.

  • 100 spa and makeup orders in 5 worlds
  • Foam cleanse, steam, peel-off masks and cucumber eyes
  • Paint lipstick, blush and eyeshadow with real brush strokes
  • Long, doll, winged, star and rainbow lashes
  • Face gems and cute face paint designs
  • Calm flute spa music and soft relaxing sounds
