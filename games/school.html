Kibbi School Day

SimulationNo adsAges 4+

A fun school day with Kibbi: pack, ride the bus, learn, play and eat lunch!

Good morning, Kibbi Town! It is a school day and Kibbi needs your help from breakfast to the last bell. Pack the backpack with everything on the school list, ride the yellow school bus and tap STOP to pick up Tobi and Zuzu, then hurry to class. The teacher has fun board games ready: count the strawberries, find the first letter of a word, spell little words, tap the right color balloons and mix paints. In the music room you listen to a tune on the xylophone and play it back, and in the art room you connect the dots and color your picture. At recess pop bubbles with Tobi, push Zuzu high on the swing or play hopscotch, fill your lunch tray like the lunch card, and jump rope in the gym. Every level is a new school day with 3 to 5 activities, and every school week brings new games: Back to School, Music and Art Week, Sports Week, Rainy Days and the School Fair. Mochi the kitten and Biscuit the puppy are there to say goodbye in the morning.

  • 100 school days in 5 school weeks
  • 10 activities: backpack, bus ride, counting, letters, colors, music, art, recess, lunch and gym
  • Learn numbers, sums, letters, words and colors while you play
  • Kibbi, Tobi, Zuzu and the teacher cheer for every right answer
  • Gentle helping hand for little players, no timers to fail
  • Happy accordion polka music and cheerful sounds
