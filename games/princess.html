Kibbi Princess Ball

SimulationNo adsAges 4+

Dress Kibbi and friends for 5 royal balls, then dance on the beat for judges!

The castle doors are open and the royal ball is about to begin! Kibbi, Tobi, Zuzu, little Mimi and their friends each bring a royal wish scroll: a pink princess gown, a heart tiara, glass slippers, a snow cape or a sparkly star wand. Open the golden wardrobe, try gowns, crowns and tiaras, jewels, shoes, magic wands, capes and wings and paint them in 16 royal colors. Princes get royal jackets, crowns and capes too. Then it is time to dance! Tap when the hearts reach the golden ring, hold the candy canes, tap both lanes at the masquerade and swipe the sparkle stars. Every great tap makes your guest spin, jump and wave. Mochi the kitten, Biscuit the puppy and Mimi hold up heart cards for the outfit and the dance. Five castle balls with 20 invitations each: Rose Garden Ball, Crystal Ice Palace, Royal Candy Ball, Moonlight Masquerade and Starlight Castle.

  • 100 royal invitations in 5 castle balls
  • Gowns, tiaras, jewels, wands, capes and masks in 16 colors
  • Tap on the beat dance with holds, two lanes and swipes
  • Cute judges with heart score cards for dress and dance
  • Gentle helping hand and kind timing for little players
  • Royal fanfare music and sparkly sounds
