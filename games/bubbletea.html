Kibbi Bubble Tea Shop

SimulationNo adsAges 4+

Make bubble tea with pearls, milk, ice and cream for Kibbi and her friends!

Kibbi's bubble tea shop is open! Kibbi, Tobi, Zuzu, little Mimi and friends from town come to the counter with an order card that shows the drink they dream of, and Mochi the kitten and Biscuit the puppy wait in line for their own tiny milk cups. Pick a cup, drag scoops of tapioca pearls, rainbow jelly, popping boba and pudding into it and watch them tumble to the bottom, tap the bucket for clinking ice, choose the tea and hold to pour it up to the line, then add milk and watch the soft milk clouds swirl. Shake the drink until it is foamy, press the sealing machine for a cute printed lid, and pick a fat straw that pokes through with a pop. Later you swirl fluffy cream tops, add drizzles, cookie crumbs and fruit, pour rainbow layers in the right order and decorate bear and kitty cups with stickers. The menu board on the wall grows with every new drink you learn. Five tea shop worlds with 20 orders each: Milk Tea Bar, Fruit Tea Garden, Shake and Seal, Cloud Cream and Rainbow Specials.

  • 100 drink orders in 5 bubble tea worlds
  • Pearls, jelly and popping boba that fall and pile up
  • Pour tea to the line, swirl milk clouds and shake it
  • Seal printed lids and pop in a fat straw
  • Cream tops, fruit slices, rainbow layers and stickers
  • Gentle city pop music and soft bubbly sounds
