Kibbi Doll House Decor

SimulationNo adsAges 4+

Decorate every room of the Kibbi family house for Kibbi and her friends!

Welcome to the Kibbi family house! Kibbi, Tobi, Zuzu, little Mimi and Biscuit the puppy each have a wish card for their room. Paint the walls in pretty colors, pick wood floors, tiles, checker or soft carpets, and drag in beds, lamps, wardrobes, pictures and rugs. Fill the kitchen with a fridge, a stove and a table, the bathroom with a bathtub, a sink and a mirror, the living room with a sofa, an armchair and a TV, Mimi's nursery with a crib, a rocking horse and a toy box, and the garden with a swing, a fountain and a dog house for Biscuit. When the room matches the wish card, its owner runs in to cheer. Five house styles with 20 rooms each: Cozy Cottage, Candy House, Beach House, Princess Castle and Treehouse.

  • 100 room orders in 5 house styles
  • Bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, living room, nursery and garden
  • Paint walls and pick floors, tiles and carpets
  • Beds, sofas, cribs, swings and dog houses in 4 colors
  • Match the wish card for 3 stars
  • Cheerful accordion cafe music
