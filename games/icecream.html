Kibbi Ice Cream Truck

SimulationNo adsAges 4+

Drive Kibbi's ice cream truck around town and serve yummy treats!

Beep beep, the ice cream truck is here! Drag Kibbi's truck along the roads of the town map to the next stop, where Tobi, Zuzu, little Mimi, Mochi the kitten, Biscuit the puppy and friends are waiting in line. Every friend shows an order card: pick the cone or cup, scoop the flavors in the right order, drizzle chocolate, caramel or strawberry sauce and drag on sprinkles, cherries, wafers, gummy bears and more. At the pool party you fill popsicle molds with juice and dip them in chocolate, and at the carnival you build giant sundaes with whipped cream swirls. Five truck stops with 20 levels each: Sunny Park, Beach Boardwalk, Candy Street, Pool Party and Sundae Carnival.

  • 100 ice cream levels in 5 town worlds
  • Drive the truck on the town map to every stop
  • Serve a queue of friends: cones, cups, scoops and sauces
  • Popsicles with juice molds and chocolate dips
  • Giant sundaes with whipped cream and a cherry on top
  • Happy organ ska music and fun truck sounds
