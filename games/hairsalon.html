Kibbi Hair Salon

SimulationNo adsAges 4+

Wash, cut, color and style hair for Kibbi, her friends and fluffy pets!

Kibbi's hair salon is open! Kibbi, Zuzu, little Mimi, Tobi and friends from town come in with an order card showing the look they want. Rub in shampoo until the hair is full of foam, rinse it with the shower, blow dry the wet hair, comb out every tangle and snip along the line for a fresh bob. Paint the hair in fun colors like pink, purple, blue or mint, pick curls, braids, buns or a ponytail, and finish with bows, flower crowns, clips and tiaras. In the last world, Mochi the kitten, Biscuit the puppy and their pet friends come for a bubbly bath, a fluffy blow dry, a trim and a pretty bow. Five salon worlds with 20 orders each: Kibbi's Friends, Snip and Style, Color Studio, Party Hair and Pet Grooming.

  • 100 hair and pet orders in 5 salon worlds
  • Foamy wash, rinse, blow dry, comb, cut, color and style
  • Paint the hair in 10 colors with real brush strokes
  • Curls, braids, buns, ponytails and pretty clips
  • A pet grooming world with Mochi, Biscuit and friends
  • Happy ukulele music and soft salon sounds
