Kibbi Baby Sister Care

SimulationNo adsAges 4+

Babysit little Mimi with big sister Kibbi: feed, bathe, play and nap!

Kibbi is babysitting her little sister Mimi today and needs your help! Tickle sleepy Mimi awake in her crib, change her on the changing table with a wipe, baby powder and a cute fresh diaper, and hold the bottle while she drinks her milk. Feed her the baby food she wants from the high chair, give her a bubble bath with a rubber duck and rinse her off, then pick a pretty romper or a cozy bunny or bear suit. Pop bubbles, stack letter blocks, shake the rattle and push the stroller through the park with Kibbi. At night, tap the twinkling stars to sing a lullaby and tuck Mimi in with a soft blanket. Five worlds with 20 days each: Good Morning, Splash Time, Yummy Lunch, Park Day and Sweet Dreams.

  • 100 babysitting days in 5 worlds
  • Wake up, diaper change, bottle and baby food
  • Bubble bath with a rubber duck
  • Rompers and cozy bunny and bear suits
  • Bubbles, letter blocks, rattle and a stroller walk
  • Lullaby stars and a soft music box tune
