Kibbi Dress Up Party

SimulationNo adsAges 4+

Dress Kibbi and her friends for 5 themed parties and win stars from the judges!

It is party time in Kibbi Town! Kibbi, Tobi, Zuzu, little Mimi and their friends are invited to the coolest parties, and they need YOU to pick their outfits. Every guest brings a party wish card: a blue heart tee, a sparkly tiara, bunny slippers or a space helmet. Open the big wardrobe, try hairstyles, tops, dresses, shoes, hats, glasses and fun extras, paint them in any color with the swatches and the rainbow slider, then tap PARTY! Mochi the kitten, Biscuit the puppy and Mimi are the judges and hold up their heart cards. Make every wish come true to win 3 stars. Five party worlds with 20 parties each: Birthday Party, Beach Party, Pajama Party, Princess Ball and Space Party.

  • 100 parties in 5 themed worlds
  • Over 90 outfit pieces with any color you like
  • Party wish cards that grow from 1 to 4 wishes
  • Cute judges with heart score cards and confetti
  • Gentle helping hand for little players
  • Happy dance music and sparkly sounds
