Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kibbi Life ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use any game published by Kibbi Life (each a "Game"), including Kibbi Life: My Town World, on the Amazon Appstore or any other store. It is a single policy for all of our Games.

1. The short version

  • No ads and no advertising SDKs.
  • No accounts, no sign up and no email collection.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting services.
  • Our Games do not collect, send or share any personal data. We never sell data.
  • Optional in-app purchases are processed by Amazon, always behind a parent gate.

2. Data stored on your device

Game progress such as your characters, outfits, decorated rooms, coins, unlocked places, stickers and sound settings is saved only on your device in the Game's private storage. The list of unlocked in-app items is also kept on the device so the Game works offline. We run no game servers and we cannot see this data. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

3. Advertising, analytics and tracking

Our Games contain no ads, no advertising identifiers, no analytics and no third party tracking of any kind. The Games play fully offline and do not contain links to websites or social networks.

4. In-app purchases (Amazon In-App Purchasing)

Some Games offer optional extra content (for example extra places, room themes or outfit packs) as one-time in-app purchases. Purchases are handled entirely by Amazon In-App Purchasing through the Amazon Appstore on your device. Amazon processes the payment and tells the Game only which items are unlocked. We never receive your name, payment details or Amazon account credentials. Prices, payment, refunds and Amazon's parental controls are governed by Amazon's own terms and privacy notice.

5. Parent gate

The shop, every purchase and Restore Purchases are protected by a parent gate (a question a grown up must answer) so children cannot buy anything by accident. On devices with Amazon Kids or parental controls, a parent may also need to approve the purchase in Amazon's own system.

6. Permissions

Our Games request no special permissions. They do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

7. Children

Our Games are made for kids and families. Because the Games do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Emails you send us

If you write to our support address, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed. We never add you to a mailing list.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Since our Games do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to export or erase on our side. Your game data stays on your device until you uninstall the Game. For questions about data held by Amazon, see Amazon's privacy notice.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the new policy on this page.

11. Contact

Kibbi Life. Questions about privacy or anything else: support@kibbilife.com.
